How it works

Think rhythm boxing meets a fantasy quest. The snacks arrive on the beat, and the story carries you through ten lands.

🥽

Open it in your headset

Go to this page in Meta Quest Browser (or any WebXR browser) and press Enter VR. Nothing to install.

🥊

Punch, duck, lean, block

Snacks march at you to the beat. Green ring: left fist. Red: right. Arrows show hooks, uppercuts and hammer fists.

🏰

Free the realm

Clear territories, rescue villagers, fill your satchel and upgrade your gauntlets. Your progress is saved to your account.

Ten lands to free

Each land has its own music, its own snacks, its own boss, and a move it leans on.

Every move works something different

Battles rotate between these, so your shoulders rest while your legs work, and the other way round.

Questions

Which headsets does it work on?

Meta Quest 2, 3, 3S and Pro in Meta Quest Browser, plus most headsets with a WebXR browser. Without a headset you can still play on a computer, tablet or phone with mouse, touch or keyboard.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in the headset's web browser. Open the page, press Enter VR, and start punching.

How does passthrough work?

If your headset can show your room (like Quest 3), you'll see a "VR with passthrough" button. The snacks, rings and cages appear in your real room instead of a fantasy land.

Is it a real workout?

It's real movement: jabs, hooks, uppercuts, hammer fists, lunges, squats, leans and blocks, at 96 to 140 beats per minute. Calorie numbers are rough estimates. If you have a health condition, check with a doctor first, warm up, and take breaks.

Can I play sitting down?

Yes. Seated mode removes ducks and lunges and keeps everything within arm's reach.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, once you have a free account. The Training Yard works without one, so you can try it first.

Is it free?

Yes. There are no ads and nothing to buy. Sprouts, treasures and upgrades are earned only by playing.

The Horde won't punch itself.

Free account, nothing to install. Try the Training Yard first if you like.
